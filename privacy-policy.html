TinyTrack — Privacy Policy

TinyTrack is a simple baby-tracking web app. This page explains what happens to your data.

Local storage

Everything you log (feedings, diaper changes, etc.) is saved directly in your browser's local storage on your own device. This data never leaves your device unless you turn on the optional Google Drive backup described below. Uninstalling the app, clearing your browser data, or switching devices/browsers will remove it, since nothing is stored on any server we control.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to sign in with Google, TinyTrack requests access only to its own hidden application-data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). This lets TinyTrack save one private backup file so your log can survive clearing local storage or be restored on another device. That file:

  • is not visible in your regular Google Drive file list,
  • is never shared with anyone else, sold, or used for advertising,
  • is never accessed by any other Google Drive file or by any human — only by the TinyTrack app code running in your own browser.

Signing out only revokes TinyTrack's access token; it does not delete your local data or your Drive backup file.

What we don't do

TinyTrack has no server, no analytics, no ad tracking, and no account system beyond the optional Google sign-in described above. We (the developer) never see, collect, or have access to your logged data.
